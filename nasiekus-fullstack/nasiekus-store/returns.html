Returns & Refunds

Not quite right? Here's how returns and refunds work at NASIEKU'S STORE.

Our policy

You may return unworn, unwashed items with tags attached within 14 days of delivery. Footwear must be returned in the original box, undamaged.

How to start a return

  1. Email simonparsalat@gmail.com with your order number and the item you'd like to return.
  2. We'll confirm eligibility and send a return address and instructions.
  3. Pack the item securely and hand it to the courier we arrange for pickup.
  4. Once received and inspected, your refund or exchange is processed within 5 business days.

Refund method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method — M-Pesa, card, or as store credit if you'd prefer. Delivery fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.
