Shipping & Delivery

Where we deliver, how long it takes, and what it costs.

Delivery areas & timelines

We currently deliver across all 47 counties in Kenya using trusted courier partners. Estimated timelines from dispatch:

  • Nairobi & environs: 1–2 business days
  • Major towns (Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret): 2–3 business days
  • Other counties: 3–5 business days

Delivery fees

  • Nairobi orders over KES 5,000: free delivery
  • Nairobi orders under KES 5,000: flat KES 350
  • Outside Nairobi: calculated at checkout based on courier rates for your area

Order processing

Orders placed before 2pm EAT on a business day are typically packed and handed to courier the same day. Orders placed after 2pm, on weekends, or on public holidays are processed the next business day.

Tracking your order

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking reference by SMS and email. You can check status any time on our Track Order page.

Questions

If a delivery is delayed beyond the estimate above, reach out on +254 748 525 386 or simonparsalat@gmail.com and we'll chase it up with the courier.
